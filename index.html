Our Story

Behind every grand feast is passion, precision & personal care

MRS Caterings was founded in 1989 by Dr. M.R. Srinivasan and continues today as Kaviarasan's MRS Caterings. In over three decades we have catered weddings, college events, corporate openings and family celebrations, and our standard has stayed the same: every guest is served with care.

From a single plate on a banana leaf to a buffet for five thousand, the taste and consistency stay the same. That is what we mean by passionate to delight.
